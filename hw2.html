PROJECT GALLERY / 2026

About This Project

For this homework, I practiced cropping photos in Photoshop. Before this exercise, I thought cropping was just changing the size of a photo. After doing the assignment, I understood that cropping can also change how we see a photo. For the first photo, I changed the size to 1080 × 1920 pixels. Because the photo had to be vertical, I had to choose what parts of the original photo to keep. I decided to keep the water bottle as the main subject. I removed some of the background so that the bottle was easier to see. This showed me that cropping can help make one object stand out more. For the second photo, I changed the size to 1920 × 1080 pixels. This made the photo horizontal. I kept the Empire State Building in the center and also kept the sky and other buildings around it. I think the wider crop works well because it shows more of New York City and the sunset. This assignment helped me understand that the size and shape of a photo are important. A vertical photo and a horizontal photo can make the same image look very different. I also learned that when we crop a photo, we have to think about what is most important in the picture. We cannot always keep everything from the original photo. Overall, I learned that cropping is not only about changing the size. It is also about choosing what the viewer will see and what will be the main focus of the photo.
