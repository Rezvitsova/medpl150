About This Project

HOMEWORK 05

For this project, I created a stylized illustration of an eye using Adobe Illustrator. My main goal was to create an expressive and visually interesting design using simple shapes, colors, and gradients. Instead of drawing a realistic eye, I wanted to experiment with abstract forms and give the illustration a more artistic and modern appearance. I used different curved shapes to create the upper and lower eyelids. Their organic forms make the composition feel more dynamic and give the eye a sense of movement. I chose a color palette that combines red, dark green, and teal. The gradient transitions between red and green create contrast and make the shapes look more dimensional. I also used a gradient inside the iris, which helps it stand out as the central element of the illustration. The black pupil creates a strong focal point and contrasts with the lighter colors around it. I decided to keep the background white because I wanted the eye to remain the main focus. The empty space around the illustration also makes the composition look clean and balanced. I added thin dark outlines to separate the shapes and make their edges more defined. One of the most interesting parts of this project was experimenting with the Pen Tool, creating custom shapes, and adjusting gradient colors. It helped me become more comfortable working with vector illustrations and understand how different shapes and colors can work together. Overall, I am happy with the result because I was able to transform a simple subject into an abstract design while maintaining the recognizable form of an eye.
