About This Project

HOMEWORK 03

My photomontage focuses on the war in Ukraine and the way people are fighting for their country. As a Ukrainian, this subject is deeply personal to me; I lived in Ukraine my entire life and left the country after the first year of the war. I witnessed how the war transformed daily life and affected the people around me. In this piece, I incorporated various images: posters bearing protest slogans, a photograph of a Ukrainian soldier, and an image of the Ukrainian flag featuring handwritten messages. I combined these elements to highlight different facets of the situation: people speaking out against the war; soldiers defending their country including one who wrote "Love Ukraine"; and ordinary citizens expressing their love for their homeland, no matter the circumstances. I selected these specific images because, together, they convey a message that is far more powerful and evocative than any single image could on its own. I created this photomontage in Photoshop using multiple image layers. To hide specific parts of the images without permanently deleting them, I utilized layer masks. I also adjusted the size and placement of the images to build the composition. To achieve a harmonious blend of elements, I applied the "Multiply" blending mode to one of the layers; this allowed the photographs to interact visually with one another rather than appearing as disjointed fragments. Additionally, I adjusted the brightness and contrast to ensure the images complemented each other effectively. My goal was to create a composition that linked my personal experience with the emotions of other Ukrainians, illustrating how people continue to support their country amidst the war.
