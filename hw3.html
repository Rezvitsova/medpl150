PROJECT GALLERY / 2026

About This Project

In this practice, I learned about Shallow Depth of Field and Deep Depth of Field. I took two photos with almost the same composition. In the Shallow DoF photo, the main subject is clear and the background is blurry. In the Deep DoF photo, more of the objects in the photo are clear and in focus. This helped me understand how the camera can change the focus of a photo. I also learned how to make my photos look better in Photoshop. I used Camera Raw, Levels, and Curves to change the brightness and contrast. I learned that small changes can make a photo look clearer and more balanced. For the last photo, I used my Deep DoF photo and made an artificial blur with the Camera Raw Filter. I kept the main subject clear and made the background blurry. This made the photo look more like it was taken with a shallow depth of field. Overall, this practice helped me understand how focus and blur work. I also learned how I can use Photoshop to change the look of a photo and make the main subject stand out.
