About This Project

HOMEWORK 06

For this project, I created a simple illustration based on a photograph of a beach. My goal was to turn a realistic landscape into a simplified vector illustration using basic shapes and a limited color palette. I wanted to keep the main details of the original photograph while making the image look cleaner and more artistic. I divided the environment into three parts: foreground, middle ground, and background. The foreground includes the sand and waves. The middle ground shows the ocean, bridge, and city skyline. The background contains the sky and clouds. Separating the image into these parts helps create depth and makes the composition easier to understand. I used simple shapes and curved lines to represent the natural forms of the beach and water. I also chose a small number of colors, including blue, dark teal, and beige, to keep the illustration consistent. The different colors help separate the main elements without adding too many details. One important part of this assignment was understanding why Image Trace should not be used. Instead of automatically converting the photograph into vector shapes, the goal was to make my own decisions about which details to keep and which ones to simplify. This allowed me to create a more personal interpretation of the original image. Through this project, I learned that creating an illustration is not just about copying a photograph. It is also about choosing shapes, colors, and details carefully. I also learned how foreground, middle ground, and background work together to create depth. Overall, this assignment helped me understand the process of transforming a real environment into a simple and clear vector illustration.
