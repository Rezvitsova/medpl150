About This Project

HOMEWORK 04

For this poster, I wanted to communicate support for Ukraine and show how a simple message can carry strong emotional meaning. I chose a photograph of a Ukrainian soldier writing “Любіть Україну” on a chalkboard. In the foreground, the Ukrainian flag covered with handwritten messages becomes the main visual focus. These details make the poster feel personal because they show real words and expressions of support rather than relying only on a symbol. The blue and yellow flag is important because its colors immediately connect the design to Ukraine. I added “Stand With Ukraine” in bright yellow so the message is easy to notice against the darker background. The serif typeface gives the text a serious, traditional feeling. I also placed blue and yellow outlined geometric shapes around the edges. Their sharp angles create movement and contrast with the photograph’s realistic texture. They frame the image without covering its meaningful details. I used a photograph with a grainy, documentary-like appearance because it makes the poster feel direct and emotional. The handwritten messages on the flag suggest that many people can share a message of hope and solidarity. The composition combines photography, typography, color, and graphic elements to guide the viewer’s attention from the soldier, to the flag, and finally to the central statement. Overall, this project helped me understand how visual design can communicate a social message without using much text. I hope the poster encourages viewers to remember Ukraine and recognize the importance of standing together in difficult times.
